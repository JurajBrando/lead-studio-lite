How it works

From spreadsheet to sent — in four steps

No setup call, no onboarding sequence of your own. Start with a file of leads and finish with replies in your inbox.

  1. 1

    Import

    Drop in a CSV, TSV or Excel file. Columns are mapped automatically, and you can correct any mapping before the leads are saved.

  2. 2

    Verify

    Emails are checked for syntax, role accounts and disposable domains. MX records are looked up with dnspython or nslookup, and an optional live SMTP probe goes further.

  3. 3

    Sequence

    Write the steps, add merge fields like {{first_name|there}}, and set a delay between each step. Then enroll the leads you want to contact.

  4. 4

    Send & reply

    The app sends from your mailbox, one email at a time, inside a sending window with a gap between messages and a daily cap. IMAP detects replies and bounces, stops the sequence and updates the lead.

Features

The parts of a cold-email tool you actually use

Import, verification, sequencing, paced sending, reply detection and a pipeline — wired together, running locally.

CSV & Excel import with auto-mapping

Bring a CSV, TSV or Excel export. Common column names are matched to names, emails, companies and custom fields automatically, and you confirm the mapping before import.

Email verification

Syntax, role accounts, disposable domains and MX records, with an optional live SMTP probe. Every lead keeps its verification result so you can filter before you send.

Multi-step sequences with merge fields

Build as many steps as you need, with merge fields like {{first_name|there}} and per-step delays. Open and click tracking use a local tracking URL, and every step can carry an unsubscribe link.

Paced sending with daily caps

Messages go out one at a time inside your sending window, with a gap between each one and a per-mailbox daily cap. The pace is yours, not a shared pool's.

Automatic reply & bounce detection

The app reads your inbox over IMAP, spots replies and bounces, stops the sequence for that lead and marks the outcome — so no one gets a follow-up after they answer.

Local pipeline + CSV export

A simple CRM view moves each lead through new → contacted → replied → interested → won or lost. Export the whole set to CSV whenever you want it, no lock-in.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription and no cloud tier. The app runs on your computer, keeps its data in a local data folder, and has no telemetry. It is the free alternative to cold-email tools that charge $50–200 a month.

Do I need Python?

For the source version, yes: Python 3.9+ on Windows. Unzip the folder and run START_LEAD_STUDIO.bat. There is also an optional .exe build with PyInstaller if you would rather not install Python.

Which mailboxes work?

Any mailbox you can send from over SMTP and read over IMAP, including Gmail, Outlook, Fastmail or a mailbox on your own domain. Use an app password rather than your main account password.

Will my emails land in spam?

No tool can guarantee inbox placement, and Lead Studio Lite is not an email warm-up service. What it does is verify addresses before you send, pace messages inside a sending window with a gap and a daily cap, and stop on replies and bounces. Domain reputation, content and how you got the list still decide where mail lands.

What about GDPR and unsubscribe?

Every sequence can include an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribed addresses go to a suppression list so they are skipped. You are responsible for having a lawful basis and following anti-spam law such as GDPR, CAN-SPAM or PECR. Because the data stays on your machine, there is no third-party email processor in the loop.
